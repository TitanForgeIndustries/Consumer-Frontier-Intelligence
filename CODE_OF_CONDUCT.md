# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Consumer Frontier Intelligence is intended to be a serious, open research project.

Participants are expected to communicate respectfully, discuss ideas on their technical merits, and distinguish evidence from speculation.

## Expected behavior

- Critique methods rather than people.
- Clearly identify assumptions.
- Support technical claims with evidence where practical.
- Report uncertainty and negative results honestly.
- Welcome good-faith disagreement.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, personal attacks, threats, discrimination, deliberate disruption, and malicious behavior are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, or restrict participation when necessary to protect a productive research environment.

Questions about enforcement can be raised privately with a project maintainer.
